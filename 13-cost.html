Why Cost Management Matters

Fabric centralizes analytics workloads, but without proper cost management, organizations may experience:

  • Unexpected consumption spikes
  • Overloaded capacities
  • Duplicate workloads increasing cost
  • Unoptimized pipelines or notebooks
  • Unused items consuming resources

Cost management ensures predictable spending and efficient resource usage.

Key Concepts

  • Capacities: Fabric uses capacity units (CU) to allocate compute.
  • Workload Optimization: SQL, Lakehouse, ML, and Real-Time workloads consume different amounts.
  • Monitoring: Track usage, peak hours, and expensive operations.
  • Governance: Prevent unnecessary duplication of datasets and pipelines.
  • Lifecycle Management: Archive or delete unused items to reduce cost.

Cost Saving Strategies

  • Use shared datasets instead of duplicates.
  • Schedule pipelines during off‑peak hours.
  • Optimize notebooks to avoid long-running sessions.
  • Use domains to reduce workspace sprawl.
  • Monitor capacity usage weekly.

Short Example

A company notices that nightly pipelines consume high compute. They optimize transformations, reduce unnecessary joins, and move some tasks to incremental refresh. Monthly cost drops significantly while performance improves.

Cost management keeps Fabric efficient and budget-friendly.
