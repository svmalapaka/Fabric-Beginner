Common Mistakes

Beginners often misunderstand Fabric because it combines many workloads into one platform. Here are the most common mistakes and how to avoid them.

❌ Mistake 1: Treating Fabric Like a Traditional Data Warehouse

Fabric is not just a SQL warehouse. It is a unified analytics platform that supports lakehouse, real-time analytics, pipelines, notebooks, and BI — all on OneLake.

❌ Mistake 2: Copying Data Between Workloads

Many beginners still copy data into separate systems for SQL, ML, and dashboards. In Fabric, you should avoid duplication — every workload reads from OneLake.

❌ Mistake 3: Ignoring Delta Format

Delta tables are the backbone of Fabric. They support ACID transactions, time travel, and efficient updates. Beginners often store raw CSVs instead — which limits performance and reliability.

❌ Mistake 4: Building Silos Inside Fabric

Even though Fabric unifies data, teams sometimes create separate workspaces that don’t share datasets. Use shared semantic models and shared lakehouses to avoid fragmentation.

❌ Mistake 5: Overusing Notebooks for Everything

Notebooks are powerful, but they are not ideal for every workload. Use pipelines for scheduled ETL, SQL for structured queries, and notebooks for ML and exploration.

❌ Mistake 6: Forgetting Governance

Fabric makes data accessible — but without proper governance, access can become chaotic. Use domains, data policies, and lineage to maintain control.
