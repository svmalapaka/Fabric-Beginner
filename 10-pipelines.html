Why This Page Matters

Beginners often confuse Pipelines, Dataflows, and Notebooks. Professionals use all three — but for different purposes.

Pipelines

Pipelines are for **ETL, automation, and scheduled data movement**.

  • Move data from external systems
  • Schedule daily or hourly refreshes
  • Orchestrate multi-step workflows
  • Ideal for batch processing

Pipelines = automation + scheduling.

Dataflows

Dataflows are **low-code data preparation** using Power Query.

  • Drag-and-drop transformations
  • Perfect for business users
  • Great for cleaning and shaping data
  • Outputs Delta tables in OneLake

Dataflows = easy transformations without code.

Notebooks

Notebooks are for **code-first data engineering and machine learning**.

  • Python, Spark, SQL
  • Feature engineering
  • ML training and scoring
  • Advanced transformations

Notebooks = full control + ML + advanced processing.

Beginner Scenario: Water Filter Example

A company selling refrigerator filters might use:

  • Pipelines to ingest daily inventory updates
  • Dataflows to clean product descriptions
  • Notebooks to train a recommendation model

Each tool plays a different role — together they form a complete workflow.

Professional Insight

Use Pipelines for automation, Dataflows for business prep, and Notebooks for engineering + ML. Fabric unifies all three under OneLake.
